# Privacy Policy for StudyWise

**Last Updated: [Current Date]**

## Introduction

StudyWise ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children under 13 years of age. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our educational mobile application.

## COPPA Compliance

StudyWise complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, grade level (for students)
- **Parent Information**: Parent email and verification for students under 13
- **Educational Data**: Learning progress, quiz responses, time spent on activities

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: App features used, session duration, learning patterns
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version (for compatibility only)

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide personalized educational content
- Track learning progress
- Communicate with parents about their child's progress
- Improve our educational algorithms
- Ensure app security and prevent misuse

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
- We use secure HTTPS connections for all data transmissions
- Data is stored locally on the device with optional cloud backup
- We implement industry-standard security measures

## Information Sharing

We do NOT:
- Sell or rent personal information
- Share data with third-party advertisers
- Use student data for advertising purposes

We MAY share information:
- With parents/guardians of student accounts
- When required by law or legal process
- To protect the safety of users

## Parental Rights

Parents have the right to:
- Review their child's personal information
- Request deletion of their child's account
- Refuse further collection of information
- Update their child's information

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@studywise.ai

## Data Retention

- Active account data is retained while the account is active
- Deleted accounts have data removed within 30 days
- Anonymous usage statistics may be retained for improvement purposes

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users of material changes via:
- In-app notifications
- Email to registered parent accounts
